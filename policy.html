Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. 30-Day Guarantee

We stand behind every product we sell. If you're not completely satisfied, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked.

2. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return:

  • Item must be returned within 30 days of purchase
  • Item must be unused and in original condition
  • Original packaging must be intact
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required

3. How to Return

To initiate a return:

  1. Email support@vibewell.com with your order number
  2. Receive return shipping label via email

Refunds are processed to the original payment method. Please allow 5-7 business days for the refund to appear in your account.

If you receive a defective product, we will replace it or provide a full refund at no cost. Contact us immediately at support@vibewell.com
